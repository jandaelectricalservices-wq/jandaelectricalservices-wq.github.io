FAQ

Common questions

Still unsure? Call or text us and we'll walk you through it.

Is the ocular inspection really free?

Yes, in selected areas. Our engineer visits, checks your existing panel and loads, and gives you an itemized quotation with no obligation.

Who handles the engineering side?

J&A is powered by a Registered Electrical Engineer, Engr. John Michael Burgos. We do load calculations, system evaluations and technical inspections, and can prepare electrical plans and related engineering services subject to applicable professional requirements.

Do you install CCTV and smart switches too?

Yes. We install CCTV cameras, cabling and DVR/NVR systems, and smart, WiFi and touch-panel switches with lighting controls and automation.

How are payments structured?

Most projects use a downpayment on approval, a progress billing during installation, final billing on turnover, and a small retention released at the end of the warranty period. Every term is shown in your client portal.

Do you handle permits and inspections?

We prepare the drawings and test documentation needed for your building administration or local permit, and coordinate the inspection with you.

What is the fastest way to reach you?

Message us on Messenger or WhatsApp, or call or text 0915-112-0470. We are on site Monday to Sunday, 8 AM to 8 PM.

Can you work around our business hours?

Yes. For restaurants, clinics and offices we schedule noisy or shutdown work for nights or weekends and agree the plan in advance.

What happens if something fails after turnover?

We give a one-year service warranty on covered workmanship, subject to applicable terms and conditions. Submit a service request in the client portal or call us, and we schedule a warranty inspection.
